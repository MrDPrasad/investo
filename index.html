RD→FD Cumulative Planner (Compound Roll-Over)

This tool helps you plan your recurring deposits (RD) rolling over into fixed deposits (FD) with compounding. It supports three modes: calculating the schedule, solving for initial deposit, or solving for annual increment. Default interest rate is set to 7% based on current RD/FD rates in India around 4.75% to 8.50% as of August 2025. You can adjust it as needed.
